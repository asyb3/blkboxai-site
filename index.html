Workshops

Three workshops · In person · No technical experience needed

Workshop 01

AI 101

The AI workshop every professional needs. We cover the essential techniques and discover tools that will transform your workflows. Participants learn to make the most out of AI for their given roles and responsibilities

For
Professionals using AI tools in their daily work.
Format
Hands-on, built around participants' own tasks.
Outcome
Practical skills and tools that augment their abilities.
01 · Context Engineering

Master the context.

Write clear instructions, supply reference material and examples, and set up reusable project contexts that produce consistent, verifiable results.

02 · Data Guidance

It's all about data.

The process of finding and curating information sources to feed the AI for data-driven results.

03 · Evaluation

Benchmark, evaluate, repeat.

Match tools to tasks, check output quality, turn one-off prompts into repeatable workflows, and recognize when a task should not go to AI at all.


Workshop 02

AI Labs

In this experimental, fully hands-on lab, teams and individuals are guided from idea or question to a working prototype or demo, learning the frontier techniques and tools to meet their outcome goals.

For
Teams or individuals with an idea, question or process to turn into a proof of concept.
Format
Experimental and guided, built around your objectives.
Outcome
A robust prototype and the techniques to build the next one.
01 · Frontier techniques

Start from what works.

A curated set of the techniques that hold up to the most advanced LLM capabilities.

02 · Building

From idea to working tool.

Guided step by step, assessing the mental frameworks and building a solid ground for decisions.

03 · Verification

Convincing is not correct.

Test what was built, understand the limitations, and map the road from a working demo to a final product.


Workshop 03

AI decisions framework

Leadership works through real organizational problems with AI: framing the question, using AI to research, analyze, and test options, then turning the findings into decisions. The workshop builds a working framework and foundational skillset leaders can use to make strategic decisions.

For
Executives and decision-makers, no technical experience assumed.
Format
Facilitated, hands-on, built on organizational case studies.
Outcome
AI augmented decisions framework.
01 · Framing

Define the right problem.

Turn a business or operational question into precise context, by questioning the assumptions and considering pertinent data sources. 

02 · Problem Solving

Work the problem with AI.

Use AI to research, model scenarios, compare options, and stress-test assumptions on live organizational problems.

03 · Decision

From analysis to decision.

Weigh AI-assisted findings against your own domain knowledge, assess the evidence, and keep judgment and accountability where they belong: with leadership.
