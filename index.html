Philosophy

Implementing highly-effective AI systems is a multifaceted process that goes far beyond optimization metrics and task automation. There is a balance to be found in integrating AI to augment operational efficiency and enable a high skill workforce, without compromising on critical reasoning or quality. The integration of AI is more than a technological challenge. It requires careful consideration of the processes the technology changes, alongside the people who's work gets reorganizes. I help teams navigate this difficult conundrum technologically and operationally, and take pride in turning complexity into comprehensible. Efficiency and speed are byproducts of that process.
